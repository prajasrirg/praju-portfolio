03 // INDUSTRIAL EXPERIENCE

Engineering Case Studies

Notes from the manufacturing floor. Two industrial internships, read chronologically — each an immersion into how a component is actually made, tested, and shipped.

01
01 — MANUFACTURING · QUALITY QA

LED Manufacturing & Quality Assurance — Luker Electric Technologies

Exposure to the manufacturing and quality assurance processes behind LED lighting products, ceiling fans, and electrical appliances — including PCB assembly, Surface Mount Technology (SMT), quality inspection, and final product testing.

A highlight was visiting NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing Laboratories), learning industrial testing procedures — lumen testing, Hi-Pot testing, insulation & leakage current testing, IP65/IP66 testing, goniophotometer beam-angle measurement, and integrating sphere photometric measurement. Time in R&D gave me insight into prototype validation and mass production.

Working alongside experienced engineers sharpened my understanding of fault analysis, troubleshooting, and quality control.

With thanks to Luker Electric Technologies Pvt Ltd and KCT for the opportunity.

02 — ELECTROMECHANICAL · MOTORS

Motor Winding & Industrial Testing — Dharani Pumps & Motors

Hands-on experience with motor technology and industrial practice, deepening my understanding of how a motor is actually built and proven.

  • Practical exposure to single-phase & three-phase induction motors.
  • Hands-on winding and soldering operations under expert supervision.
  • Procedures for type testing and routine testing of motors.
  • Manual testing methods and formulas used for motor diagnostics.
  • The varnishing process and insulation techniques essential for motor durability.
  • Real-time industrial workflows and quality control alongside factory professionals.

This internship strengthened my foundational knowledge in electromechanical systems and sparked a deeper interest in motor control, embedded systems, and industrial automation.
