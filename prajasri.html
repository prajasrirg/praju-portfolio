Engineering Case Studies

Notes from the manufacturing floor.

Two industrial internships, read chronologically — each an immersion into how a component is actually made, tested, and shipped.

01

LED Manufacturing & Quality Assurance

Industrial Intern — Luker Electric Technologies

Exposure to the manufacturing and quality assurance processes behind LED lighting products, ceiling fans, and other electrical appliances — including PCB assembly, Surface Mount Technology (SMT), quality inspection, and final product testing.

A highlight was visiting NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories), where I learned industrial testing procedures — lumen testing, Hi-Pot testing, insulation and leakage current testing, IP65/IP66 testing, beam-angle measurement using a goniophotometer, and photometric measurement using an integrating sphere. Time in the R&D department gave me a look at prototype development, product validation, and the path from design to mass production.

Working alongside experienced engineers and technicians sharpened my understanding of fault analysis, troubleshooting, and quality control — and how different manufacturing units coordinate while holding a consistent quality standard.

With thanks to Luker Electric Technologies Pvt Ltd and Kumaraguru College of Technology for the opportunity.

02

Motor Winding & Industrial Testing

Industrial Intern — Dharani Pumps and Motors, Kalapatti

Hands-on experience with motor technology and industrial practice, deepening my understanding of how a motor is actually built and proven.

  • Practical exposure to the construction and working principles of single-phase and three-phase induction motors.
  • Hands-on winding and soldering operations under expert supervision.
  • Procedures for type testing and routine testing of motors.
  • Manual testing methods and formulas used for motor diagnostics.
  • The varnishing process and insulation techniques essential for motor durability.
  • Real-time industrial workflows and quality control, alongside experienced engineers and factory professionals.

This internship strengthened my foundational knowledge in electromechanical systems and sparked a deeper interest in motor control, embedded systems, and industrial automation.
